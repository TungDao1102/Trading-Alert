## Verified Safe Areas
- **Timestamp / recvWindow / drift limits** → compared `binance-limits-v1.toml` (recvWindow 5000, ahead 500, behind 1000) with the official inequality → `f_general.md:210-224` → the ahead limit (500 ms) is inside the exchange's 1000 ms, the behind limit (1000 ms) is inside recvWindow; holds.
- **The SDK cannot silently re-send an order** → read `binance_common/utils.py:324-338,455-545` → the SDK retries only GET/DELETE and only when `retries > 0`; the project sets `sdk_retries = 0`; a POST is sent once. A `requests` timeout becomes `NetworkError` → `STATUS_UNKNOWN`; a non-JSON 2xx body raises a non-SDK exception → `UNKNOWN_EXCHANGE_ERROR`. Both ambiguous, never "absent".
- **"Unknown" after a timeout / 5xx / 408 / -1006 / -1007** → matches the official text that execution status is unknown (F07-F09). The project never reads these as "not placed".
- **Conditional orders go to the Algo endpoints with explicit `MARK_PRICE`** → `binance_usdm.py:1564-1579` vs OpenAPI default `CONTRACT_PRICE` (F31); closePosition is never sent with quantity or reduceOnly (F32).
- **Client ids** → 36 characters, charset inside the documented regex (F34); "unique among open orders" is read correctly: the project does not rely on the exchange to deduplicate (F12, EXECUTION_SPEC §7.1).
- **GTD** → the project keeps 900 s of remaining validity against the exchange's 600 s floor and does not treat goodTillDate as proof of a terminal order (F37, F38).
- **Not-found windows** → the 3-day rule used by restart recovery is the documented one, and it now also holds for algo orders (F35, F36).
- **User-data lifecycle** → listenKey 60 min / keepalive 30 min, 24 h connection, `listenKeyExpired`, ordering only inside one event type, ALGO_UPDATE vocabulary: all match the official page character for character (F19-F26). TRIGGERING/TRIGGERED/FINISHED/CANCELED classed UNKNOWN is stricter than the documentation.
- **Decimal on the wire** → `_decimal_to_wire` sends fixed-point strings although the SDK annotates `float` (`binance_usdm.py:678-690`); no float conversion on the order path.
- **Income paging** → the adapter pages by time cursor, so the removal of `page` on 2026-10-16 does not affect it (F46).

### G-2201
- **ID**: G-2201 · **Severity**: LOW (now, placement disabled) / HIGH (mainnet with placement enabled) · **Confidence**: PROBABLE (the facts are confirmed in the project's own evidence and the official change-log; the feasibility of the alternative rests on Demo samples only) · **Classification**: DESIGN

## Owner decisions
1. **Stop before or after the fill (G-2201).** Today: the stop is placed after the fill, by the running process. Alternative shown feasible on Demo: a reduce-only STOP_MARKET with the planned quantity placed together with the entry. Example: long 0.004 BTC at 80,000, stop 1.5% away (risk 4.8 USDT). If the VPS is offline when the entry fills and price drops 6% before it returns, today's model loses about 19.2 USDT with no stop on the exchange; with a pre-placed stop the exchange exits near -4.8 USDT. Cost of the alternative: an orphan stop to cancel when the entry never fills, and reliance on reduce-only clipping after a partial fill (one Demo sample). The owner chooses; mainnet behaviour of the pre-placed stop is not observed.
2. **May a reduce-only close be sent again after a certain refusal (G-2203)?** Today: one close attempt; any answer that is not in the table, and every 5xx, freezes it as UNKNOWN. Example: emergency close refused with -4131 in a fast market → no second attempt → position open without a stop until the owner reacts. Option A: keep (never two closes, accept the wait). Option B: allow a new close under a new id after a documented definitive refusal. Option C: B plus after N "not found" polls. A second reduce-only close cannot open a position, but B/C change D080.
3. **418 with no Retry-After: wait 3 days? (D072)** The documented ban range is 2 minutes to 3 days; neither the header nor the ban-end format is documented for futures. Example: first real ban is 2 minutes, the body does not match the 13-digit pattern → the bot blocks its own REST for 3 days (no reconcile, no close) unless the owner runs `rest_gate_cli clear`. Options: keep 3 days (safest against a longer ban, needs the owner within hours), or a shorter fallback with an escalating re-check. No number is proposed here.
4. **Re-read the exchange facts with exact sources (G-2202).** Decide whether the Phase 25 gate requires the facts register to be re-read from `llms-full.txt` and the OpenAPI document (character-exact) instead of summarised fetches.
