## G-1400
- Outcome: DOWNGRADED. Severity now LOW (placement disabled, Demo only); severity mainnet MEDIUM (claimed: MEDIUM now, HIGH mainnet).
- Independent evidence (snapshot 55885b9):
  - The claim about the fake itself is TRUE. `tests/support/fake_binance_exchange.py:265-317` `fill()` moves `self.positions` in the same call (`_apply_position_delta`, line 314). `place_entry` (471-511) and `place_algo_protective` (513-563) put the record in `open_orders`/`open_algos` at once, and `query_order_by_client` (565-587) / `query_algo_order_by_client` (589-614) find it on the next call. The only built-in lag is `algo_cancel_query_lag` (201-207, 342-348, 603-611). It defaults to `timedelta(0)` and is used only by `tests/test_protection_cancel_lag.py` and `tests/test_sweeper_cancel_lag.py`, plus the fake itself (so the "3 files" figure is right).
  - 28 `test_*.py` files and 3 support modules import the fake directly. Counting indirect use through `protection_doubles`, `live_rig` and similar, about 68 test files touch it. The claim's "30" is about right.
  - The part that is REFUTED is "by-id queries find an order immediately after placement; only a post-cancel algo lag exists". Several test files wrap the fake's methods to model lag:
    - `tests/test_protection_demo_shapes.py:40-71`: `DemoLag(by_id_absent, listing_hides)` makes the by-client-id algo query answer found=False (the -2013 shape) right after placement while the stop rests NEW, and lets the open-algo listing omit the stop. The docstring cites Demo evidence of 2026-10-22, 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-15. Used at lines 102-105, 261-274, 321 and 379.
    - `tests/test_protection_deadline_attempt.py` imports `DemoLag` and `_first_fill_step`, and runs the same lag across the deadline step.
    - `tests/test_execution_attach.py:511-524`: `test_a_stop_not_found_once_then_active_is_adopted_on_the_reread` (D113, answers ABSENT then ACTIVE). Lines 716-740: a take-profit (TP) leg placed but not found by REST, with three re-reads ending UNKNOWN. Around line 1052: only the first confirmation read lags.
    - A delayed position after a fill is modelled as one transient empty read: `tests/support/protection_doubles.py:323-340` `script_empty_position_reads`. It is used in `tests/test_protection_branches.py:493` and `:544`. The second of these, `test_branch_no_position_4509_one_empty_position_read_keeps_the_plan_unprotected`, is "fill seen, stop answers -4509, the first position read is empty although the position is open", which is exactly delayed position after fill. It is also used in `tests/test_protection_take_profits.py:455` and `:473`, and in `tests/test_emergency_market_close.py:475` and `:500`.
    - A normal order missing by id: scripted found=False probes after an ambiguous send in `tests/test_execution_manager.py:1047-1155` and `:1427-1554`, and in `tests/test_emergency_market_close.py:600` and `:777`. These paths go fail-closed (UNKNOWN plus SAFE_MODE, no re-send).
- What remains a real gap:
  1. Lag is not a default property of the fake. Most of the ~68 dependent files run with perfect read-after-write consistency, so a new code path gets no lag coverage unless its author scripts it.
  2. A position lag longer than one read is not modelled. The coordinator treats two zero reads in a step as FLAT (`test_tp_exit_needs_two_consecutive_zero_reads`, and `test_branch_stop_consumed_flat_reads_the_position_twice_before_flat`). No test checks a case where both reads come back empty after a real fill, for example in the -4509 branch. On mainnet that could leave an open position marked FLAT with its legs swept. Reconcile is the later backstop, and how often this happens is unproven.
  3. A normal LIMIT entry or MARKET close is never shown missing by id shortly after a SUCCESSFUL acknowledgment, only after an ambiguous send. The fake's `place_market_close` fills at once, or rests with `market_close_rests`.
  4. The lag values used are TEST_ONLY, shaped on Demo and never measured on mainnet.
- Counter-checks performed: grepped the snapshot tests for `algo_cancel_query_lag`, -2013, `found=False`, re-read/reread, overrides of `snapshot_positions` and `query_order_by_client`, and `DemoLag`/`script_empty_position_reads`/`script_stop_answers`. I read the bodies of the fake, the demo-shape tests, the attach re-read tests, the -4509 branch tests and the TP transient-read tests. In `src/reconcile/loop.py` and `src/execution/protection.py` I found only GTD-plus-grace handling, no general read-lag tolerance.
- Notes: I did not verify that the escaped defects G-0250, G-0253 and G-0300 share this cause; that would need those findings' locations, and I did not open `lanes/`. Severity is lowered because the lag shapes actually seen on Demo (algo by-id -2013 after NEW, listing omission, cancel lag, one empty position read) all have scripted tests. What is left is the absence of systematic or default lag and of lag lasting several reads. That gap can hide fail-open defects in protection on mainnet (MEDIUM), but it is a coverage gap, not a defect that has been shown.
