<objective>
Close the B1 binding hole: only registry-consistent cost models are assemblable into experiment records.

Purpose: Today assemble() binds the registry set's parameter_set_hash and the caller CostModel's cost_model_hash into one identity without proving they came from the same cost assumptions, so an experiment can silently attest to untested costs. The fail-closed gate makes parameter set + cost a verified pair, hardening BKTS-02 reproducibility.
Output: Binding helper plus exception, gate wired into assemble(), two 7-key baseline-v3 registry sets, phase7.1 golden fixtures, gate test module, migrated assemble()-calling tests, extended static guards.

Assumption-delta (advisory, per phase brief): registry TOML stays the primary authority and the cost dict stays a verified snapshot alongside it (promote/keep-primary, not dedup) — rationale: fixtures need self-contained cost snapshots for byte-exact replay without a registry checkout, and simulator.run must stay decoupled from src/domain/registry.py (per D-02).
</objective>
