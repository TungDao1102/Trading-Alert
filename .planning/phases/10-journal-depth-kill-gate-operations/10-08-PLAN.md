<objective>
Compose the phase's pure dashboard model: `build_journal_depth_report` assembles per-evidence-kind analytics blocks, the kill-gate section (verdict + per-family independence and gate cells), the INCOMPLETE ledger, provenance and live posture into one digest-stamped `JournalDepthReport` that the renderer (plans 10 and 11 of this phase) consumes.

Purpose: D-01 wants one digest per report so identical inputs give byte-identical output; D-05 wants full provenance and unpooled evidence; D-04 wants both universes and the kill-gate state together. The model is the stable contract beneath the operator-facing layout (Reversibility costly for provenance fields, which become the audit trail future amendments cite).
Output: `report.py`, `provenance.py`, composite fixture and tests.
