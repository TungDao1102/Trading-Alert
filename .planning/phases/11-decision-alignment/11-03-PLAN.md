<objective>
Ship the point-in-time research universe of D039 as a committed, machine-validated manifest in DRAFT state (D-09): the rule and the slots are recorded and validated now, and the freeze-and-hash step is handed to Phase 13, which will have the backfilled volume data needed to rank symbols at the dataset start date.

Purpose: D039 fixes the research universe as the top 10-15 USDT perpetuals by volume at the dataset start date, delisted symbols included, frozen and hashed before evaluation. The volume data does not exist yet, so listing symbols now would be hand-picking (survivorship bias, the very defect D039 fixes). The CONTEXT default path params/universe/ is unsafe (11-RESEARCH F7): three consumers would read it as a strategy family, so the manifest lives under docs/research/universe/.
Output: DRAFT manifest, validator/hasher module with a CLI, tests.
