Regenerate the Phase 6 simulator and baseline fixtures with a realistic candle geometry and the Phase 12 parameter sets, so that when the cost guards go live (plan 12-06) these fixtures still yield real trades. The old generator builds a +0.10 per bar drift with 0.05 half-range bars, giving stops 0.06 to 0.26 percent from entry, which the new minimum-stop and cost-to-risk gates would reject (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).

Output: regenerated fixtures, a pre-flight geometry test, updated baseline tests.

Planning-time prototype (existing planner and strategy, params baseline-v4 which equals v5 for non-cost keys; the executor MUST re-verify by running the pre-flight test): with 1H half-range 0.80 the LONG trigger bar (open 121.50, high 125.00, low 121.50, close 124.50) gives entry zone 120.4586 to 122.5414, stop 121.1529, TP1 125.60 (stop about 1.133 percent of the zone-top); the SHORT trigger bar (open 128.50, high 128.50, low 125.00, close 125.00) gives zone 127.459 to 129.541, stop 128.847, TP1 124.40 (about 1.089 percent from zone-bottom); baseline-simple-trend-v2 geometry gives a plan with stop about 1.83 percent; baseline-no-trade gives RR_TOO_LOW (no plan, as designed). Decisive 1m candles must therefore be: LONG low 121.10 (below the stop) and high 125.70 (above TP1); SHORT high 128.90 (above the stop) and low 124.30 (below TP1). Independent arithmetic at those numbers: LONG cost at the zone-top is 122.5414 x 0.0006 + 121.1529 x 0.0008 = 0.170447, risk_per_unit 1.5589, ratio about 0.109 (limit 0.15).
