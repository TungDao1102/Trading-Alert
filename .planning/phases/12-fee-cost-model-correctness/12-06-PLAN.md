<objective>
Cut the shared risk pipeline over to the corrected cost math: one producer inside evaluate_risk, sizing at the adverse edge of the entry zone, the two cost guards before quantization, and a sizing result that records the rates it used.

Purpose: this is the heart of RISK-01 (F-001, F-801, F-962, F-002): "loss at stop is at most allowed_risk" must hold for a fill anywhere in the zone, with the same function in backtest, paper and live. Proof of worst case: for LONG the SL loss for a fill p is q x [p(1 + c_e) - S(1 - c_x)], increasing in p, so the edge entry_max bounds every fill; SHORT mirrors it at entry_min; at the edge the loss equals q x risk_per_unit, and q is floored below allowed_risk / risk_per_unit.
Output: rewritten gating, a guards module, extended SizedResult, oracle and boundary tests.

Reversibility note (REVERSIBILITY_GATES on, unattended run): the adverse-edge sizing entry (D-03) is rated one-way because it changes every sized quantity and golden fixture. No blocking checkpoint is inserted in this unattended --auto run; the decision is recorded in DECISIONS D046 by plan 12-11 (supersession of the Phase 5 D-02 zone-mid rule as the risk-sizing entry; zone mid stays the notional, limit and simulated-fill reference).
