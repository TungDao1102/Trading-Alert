<objective>
Tracer slice of the causal backtest: one hand-built LONG TradePlan created at the close of bar N rests from bar N+1, fills on a one-tick trade-through of the tick-snapped limit on a trade-price minute, exits at the stop on a mark-price minute, and becomes a validated v2 TradeRecord whose realized_risk comes from the single R-denominator function. The phase constants, including the user-resolved TP1 policy (D-27), are created here so every later plan builds on them.

Purpose: prove the new architecture (lazy minute source, pure window-parameterized lifecycle driver, record builder, temporal validator) end to end on the best early-context tokens before entry, exit, funding, ledger and engine work expands it.
Output: seven new src/backtest modules, src/risk/realized_risk.py, TradeRecord moved to records.py with a re-export, and the lifecycle, validator, series, purity and R-function tests.
</objective>

## Interfaces (contract every later Phase 14 plan builds on)

Names and signatures below are the contract. Later plans extend bodies; they do not rename or reshape these.

- `src/backtest/semantics.py` (constants only, no logic): `SIMULATOR_SEMANTICS_VERSION = "2.0.0"`; `TP1_QUANTITY_FRACTION = Decimal("0.5")`; `POSITION_MODE = "ONE_WAY"`; `TIE_BREAK = "STOP_FIRST"`; `FUNDING_CONVENTION = "MARK_NOTIONAL_SIGNED_V1"`; `FUNDING_MAX_CADENCE = timedelta(hours=8)`; `FUNDING_JITTER = timedelta(seconds=60)`; `STRATEGY_WINDOW_BARS = 1000`; `CONTEXT_WINDOW_BARS = 300`; entry kinds `ENTRY_FILLED`, `PARTIAL_FILL`, `EXPIRED`, `INVALIDATION_CANCEL`, `DAILY_STOP_CANCEL`, `PENDING_AT_DATA_END`, `REJECT` collected in frozenset `ENTRY_KINDS`; exit kinds `SL`, `TP2`, `END_OF_DATA` in frozenset `EXIT_KINDS`; `NO_TRADE_CONFLICT = "CONFLICT_WITH_OPEN_POSITION"`; plan sources `PLAN_SOURCE_STRATEGY = "STRATEGY"` and `PLAN_SOURCE_SCRIPTED = "SCRIPTED"`. `TOUCH_NO_FILL` is a per-plan counter, never a terminal kind.
- `src/backtest/series.py`: `MinuteSeriesError(ValueError)`; `MinuteSource` (typing.Protocol) with `trade_minutes(symbol, start_exclusive, end_inclusive) -> Sequence[Candle]` and `mark_minutes(symbol, start_exclusive, end_inclusive) -> Sequence[Candle]`, both selecting minutes whose `close_time_utc` lies in `(start_exclusive, end_inclusive]`, ascending, holes simply absent; `ListMinuteSource(trade: Mapping[str, Sequence[Candle]], mark: Mapping[str, Sequence[Candle]])` validating every series at construction; `validate_minute_series(candles, *, symbol, kind)`; `parse_minute_row(row, *, kind)` where kind is `"trade"` or `"mark"` (mark rows carry no volume: the parsed Candle gets `Decimal("0")`).
- `src/backtest/entry.py`: `snapped_limit_price(*, plan, direction, price_tick_size) -> Decimal` (live `derive_limit_price` then `quantize_price` with `entry_limit_rounding`); `is_trade_through(*, minute, limit_price, price_tick_size, direction) -> bool`.
- `src/backtest/exits.py`: frozen `ProtectiveLevels(sl_trigger, tp1_trigger, tp2_trigger, tp1_quantity)` (TP fields may be None until plan 14-07); `sl_trigger_price(*, plan, direction, price_tick_size) -> Decimal` (live `derive_sl_trigger_price` then `quantize_price` with `sl_trigger_rounding`); `is_sl_touched(*, mark_minute, sl_trigger, direction) -> bool`.
- `src/backtest/lifecycle.py`: frozen `PlanState` (symbol, direction, plan, signal_close_utc, limit_price, price_tick_size, planned_quantity, fill_quantity, sized_realized_risk, levels, phase in RESTING/OPEN/CLOSED, entry_price, entry_time_utc, remaining_quantity, tp1 leg fields, touch_no_fill_count, gap_through_stop, fallback_applied, coverage_note, entry_kind, exit_kind, exit_price, exit_time_utc, resolved_at_utc); frozen `LifecycleEvent(kind, symbol, trade_plan_id, time_utc, price, quantity, entry_kind, exit_kind)` with kind in FILL/TP1/EXIT/CANCEL/PENDING; `new_plan_state(...) -> PlanState`; `advance(state, *, source, start_exclusive, end_inclusive, bar) -> tuple[PlanState, tuple[LifecycleEvent, ...]]`, pure, where `bar` is the strategy bar closing at `end_inclusive` or None when the window ends mid-bar (a cancellation instant). The engine may re-advance a saved state over a shorter window; same input gives the same output.
- `src/backtest/records.py`: `TradeRecord` (moved unchanged first 26 fields, then trailing defaulted fields `signal_close_utc`, `planned_quantity`, `tp1_quantity`, `tp1_price`, `tp1_time_utc`, `resolved_at_utc`, `touch_no_fill_count: int = 0`, `gap_through_stop: bool = False`, `constraints_extrapolated: bool = False`, `regime: str | None = None`, `funding_event_count: int = 0`); `booking_for_event(event, *, state, cost_model) -> Booking` and frozen `Booking(time_utc, symbol, trade_plan_id, amount, kind)`; `build_trade_record(*, state, strategy_version, parameter_set_hash, cost_model, funding_amount, funding_event_count, regime, constraints_extrapolated) -> TradeRecord`. `funding_amount` is the signed D-14 funding PnL (positive means received); `realized_pnl = gross_pnl - entry_fee - exit_fee - slippage_amount + funding_amount`.
- `src/backtest/validator.py`: `TradeRecordInvariantError(Exception)`; `validate_trade_record(record, *, data_start_utc=None, data_end_utc=None) -> None`.
- `src/risk/realized_risk.py`: `realized_risk_for_fill(*, sized_realized_risk, planned_quantity, filled_quantity) -> Decimal`.
