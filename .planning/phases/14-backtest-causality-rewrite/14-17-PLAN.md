## Design (Claude's discretion, recorded here; nothing here chooses a trading value)

- **Annotate-only is the only non-decision choice.** The review proposed extending the closing-bar scan back to the earliest un-scanned minute. That would make a hole resolve through a bar range, but an hour in which the symbol has no strategy bar has no bar to fall back on, and any other treatment (reprice, skip, invalidate, count in fallback_count, lower the coverage percent) changes evidence or money. This plan records the hole in the existing `coverage_note` text and changes nothing else.
- **Detection by minute continuity, not by an expected-minute grid.** While a position is open, `advance` already walks the present mark minutes of the window in order. A hole is exactly a gap of more than one minute between two consecutive walked minutes, or between the minute before the fill minute and the first walked minute. This needs no extra read of the source, no window-boundary or close-offset convention, and gives the same notes however windows are cut.
- **State.** One new frozen-dataclass field `mark_settled_through_utc: datetime | None = None` (last field of PlanState): the open time of the last mark minute whose coverage is settled, meaning walked minute by minute, or resolved by a strategy-bar fallback, or (at the fill) the minute before the fill minute. It never moves backward. It is internal: records.py reads named PlanState fields only, so no TradeRecord, run_summary key, ExperimentRecord field or digest changes.
- **Not touched:** src/backtest/engine.py, records.py, report.py, exits.py, semantics.py, the case-input schema, params/, tests/fixtures/, docs/research/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md (frozen v2.0.0) and every pinned digest. `SIMULATOR_SEMANTICS_VERSION` stays 2.0.0 because nothing the simulator prices or books changes; a bump would rewrite `run_summary` in every pinned output.
- **Known and accepted:** a window that ends mid-bar of the symbol's own bar series (only scripted fixtures and the partial window before a data end produce one in the engine) can leave a skip note, and the later closing-bar scan of the same bar can add its fallback note for the same hole. Both statements are true; no de-duplication is attempted. A test pins it.
- **Blast radius, measured by the planner with a scratch prototype of this design (not committed):** the 16 goldens, `tests/test_backtest_simulator.py` regression digests, determinism, chain, report, experiment, run-summary, records, analytics, paper and parity suites, the phase 6 fixture suites and the smoke CLI test all passed unchanged. Exactly one existing assertion changes: `tests/test_backtest_exits.py::test_a_window_that_ends_mid_bar_never_resolves_a_hole` compares the whole state with `mid == open_state`, and the settle marker now advances from 11:59 to 12:14 in that window.

## Open question for the user (OQ-1, NOT decided by this plan)

A trade held through unresolved mark holes is annotated but still counts as a normal trade: `fallback_applied`, `fallback_count`, the report's intrabar coverage percent and the experiment record do not change. Whether such a trade should instead count against intrabar coverage, be excluded from pooled R, or invalidate the run is a money and evidence choice (it needs a typed counter, a run_summary key, an ExperimentRecord field and a deliberate digest regeneration). The plan records the question in deferred-items.md and in the SUMMARY; recommended default if the user does not answer: keep annotate-only until the Phase 15 research runner decides the evidence format. Informational, not a question: no non-test experiment record exists (D052 voids all earlier evidence, no runner calls assemble outside tests), so the changed note text of a hole-bearing run cannot invalidate a stored record today.
