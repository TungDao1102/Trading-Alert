## Interface contract (later plans build on these names)
- `pivot_at(candles: Sequence[Candle], i: int, *, lookback: int, left: int, right: int, high: bool) -> Decimal | None`: most recent confirmed pivot with index t in [max(left, i - lookback + 1), i - right], scanning t downward from i - right; pivot low at t iff low[t] <= low[j] for j in [t-left, t-1] and low[t] < low[j] for j in [t+1, t+right] (mirror for highs with >= and >); raises ValueError when left < 1, right < 1, lookback < right + 1 or i is outside [0, len(candles) - 1].
- `recent_pivots(candles, i, *, lookback, left, right, high, count: int) -> tuple[tuple[int, Decimal], ...]`: up to `count` confirmed pivots (index, level), newest first, same window and tie rule.
- `geometry_version(parameter_set: ParameterSet) -> int | None` in src/core/strategy.py: the optional registry integer (None = legacy behavior).
- Registry: `GEOMETRY_VERSION_VALUES = frozenset({1})`, `TP_MODE_VALUES = frozenset({"T_MID", "T_OPPOSITE"})`.
