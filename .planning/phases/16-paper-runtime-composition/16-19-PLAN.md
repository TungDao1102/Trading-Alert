<objective>
Build the simulated fill producer with the same fill semantics as Phase 14 (SC3, D-14, L-02): the SHADOW driver re-expresses the engine's per-plan sizing sequence and drives the public lifecycle functions over live 1m trade and mark minutes; nothing is copied and the simulator semantics version stays authoritative. If a simulator change turns out to be needed, work stops and a new decision is recorded (L-02).

Source of live 1m bars (D-14 research item, decided here as the planner's implementation choice with sources): REST polling for both trade and mark 1m bars through the public adapter. Reasons: the installed SDK has no mark-price KLINE stream (research D9); its existence on the exchange is UNVERIFIED (G4, read 2026-10-08); REST markPriceKlines and klines are documented with weights 1-2 per call (docs/SOURCES.md Phase 13 rows; G2). The poll cadence and the minute close lag are timing numbers owned by the user (O-07) and are required inputs.

Conflict surfaced for the user (research Q-B): backtest fills from the signal close; a human confirms later. Task 2 is a blocking-human decision.
Output: fill driver, live minute source, tests, Q-B record.
</objective>
