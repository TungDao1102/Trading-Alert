<objective>
Replace the fixed 30 s approval lag by the measured delay wherever a human acted (D-15; SC3 "approval delay is measured, not a fixed 30 s"), carry the SHADOW record fields PAPER-02 needs ("signal/approve/order/fill timestamps", REQUIREMENTS PAPER-02) and produce the system vs user-executed-sim vs opportunity attribution for every SHADOW outcome (D-15 with the existing `AttributionKind`).

Planner choice recorded for research Q-K (how measured delay is represented; Claude's discretion because it chooses a representation, not a threshold or a semantic): extend `PaperRecord` with optional fields (`mode`, `approval_requested_at_utc`, `approval_acted_at_utc`, `confirm_requested_at_utc`, `confirmed_at_utc`, `test_only`) that are omitted from the canonical output when None, instead of a new evidence kind. This keeps D-18 "no new metric definitions", keeps every existing `paper_records_digest` pin, and keeps the 30 s constant only as the documented default for records with no human action (D-15). Rejected: a separate evidence kind (a second loader path and new report labels). Reversibility: costly once soak records exist (the record shape is read by the exporter and analytics).

Purpose: the analytics path (plan 16-36) and the running SHADOW chain (plan 16-31) need records that carry measured timestamps without disturbing the Phase 7 to 10 pins.
Output: record fields, assembler change, attribution mapping, loader acceptance, tests.
</objective>
