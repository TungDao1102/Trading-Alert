<objective>
Expand the tracer's migration 0004 into the full schema-integrity revision: column widths that fit real values, enum and quantity CHECKs, the unique and partial-unique indexes that make approvals and client ids DB-enforced, RESTRICT foreign keys, append-only and terminal-state triggers, and the two remaining new tables (plan_constraint_snapshots, system_state_events). ## Decisions applied in this plan

- C4 (orchestrator resolution): `> 0` for plan prices (entry_min_price, entry_max_price, stop_loss_price), risk_rate, risk_budget, fill_price, fill_quantity; `>= 0` for orders.quantity (close-position legs carry 0), trade-plan quantity fields and fills.commission; orders.price and orders.stop_price `IS NULL OR > 0`. Refinement recorded for D051: positions.quantity gets no sign CHECK, because src/adapters/binance_usdm.py (around line 329) documents position_amt as signed (negative for SHORT in one-way mode with position_side BOTH), so `>= 0` would reject a legal short.
- Research A3: no CHECK on orders.order_role (free-string domain field) or trade_plans.quantization_decision (tests use the non-enum value ACCEPT).
- C5: widen symbol_metadata.contract_type AND symbol_metadata.status to 32; METADATA_TEXT_MAX_LEN derived from the model; tests/test_metadata.py rewritten consistently in the same task.
- C6: 18 existing indexes (0001: 2, 0002: 13, 0003: 3), not 14.
- Terminal sets for the triggers come from the research draft transition tables, accepted as the auto-mode default (C3): signals {APPROVED, SKIPPED_USER, SKIPPED_BUSY, EXPIRED, INVALIDATED}; trade_plans {EXPIRED, INVALIDATED, SUPERSEDED}; orders.local_status {FILLED, CANCELED, EXPIRED, REJECTED}; positions {CLOSED}; approvals.status {APPROVED, SKIPPED, EXPIRED}; execution_intents.intent_status {ACKED, REJECTED}. Plan 10 cross-checks these against src/domain/transitions.py in PostgreSQL.
- ORM strategy: every NEW 0004 constraint and index is declared with `.ddl_if(dialect="postgresql")` (verified on SQLAlchemy 2.0.54) so SQLite `create_all` fixture suites (analytics, mappers, collector) keep their existing sample data; the 18 pre-existing indexes are declared plainly because they already exist in 0001-0003.
