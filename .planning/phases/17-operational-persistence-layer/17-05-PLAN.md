<objective>
Make plan_hash reproducible from the stored row (F-410): quantize every persisted plan Decimal to the NUMERIC(38,18) scale before hashing, and hash the three Decimal inputs through one context-independent canonical text so PostgreSQL's fixed-scale read-back hashes the same. Regenerate the plan-derived fixtures deliberately and inventory them.

Purpose: D-12. Today the planner computes at precision 28 and real plans carry 23-27 fractional digits; PostgreSQL rounds them silently to 18 and returns fixed-scale text, so a plan read back from its row hashes differently and the approval binding cannot be re-verified after a restart.
Output: helper move and quantization in the planner, updated planner tests, regenerated fixtures with an inventory.

## Decisions applied in this plan

- Open Q6 (orchestrator resolution): ROUND_HALF_EVEN to 18 fractional digits in the planner; move the D047 canonical decimal helper into src/domain/canonical.py; list every regenerated fixture in D051 as in D048.
- Quantization point: prices are quantized where the geometry is final, downstream sizing is computed from the quantized prices, sizing outputs are quantized, and only then are the JSON text fields built and the hash computed, so the stored row is self-consistent.
- The store-boundary PrecisionError guard and the PostgreSQL round-trip test over real planner outputs are plan 10.
