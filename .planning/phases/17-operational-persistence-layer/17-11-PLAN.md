## Decisions applied in this plan

- Open Q2 (orchestrator resolution): stored-outcome lookup before expiry, after identity.
- Open Q1: pre-placement NO_TRADE never consumes (unchanged from plan 01).
- SL write-ahead failure policy (planner decision, recorded for D051): if the SL intent cannot be written because the database is unavailable after the entry is acknowledged, enter SAFE_MODE with reason DB_FAILURE (already in GLOSSARY 9.4) and still place the SL by its deterministic algo id; the SL is never skipped for the sake of the audit row (EXECUTION_SPEC section 6, D034).
- SL result mapping: confirmed -> ACKED; not confirmed -> UNKNOWN (the existing ENTRY_WITHOUT_PROTECTIVE path enters SAFE_MODE and cancels the entry; reconciliation resolves the leg).
- Event loop: store calls stay synchronous inside the async manager (C12, Pitfall 10); no asyncio.to_thread here (Phase 24 owns the call shape).
