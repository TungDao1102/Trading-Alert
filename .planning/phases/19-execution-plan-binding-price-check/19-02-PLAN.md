<objective>
Build the pure binding core of criterion 1: what the human approved is exactly what will be placed. One function re-derives the plan hash, takes symbol and side from the persisted Signal bound by plan.signal_id, cross-checks them against the geometry, the plan constraint snapshot, the approval and the caller, and three small checks guard the quantity (D-02), the snapped geometry (D-03) and the working type (D-04). The manager wires these in plan 19-13; attach_protectives_to_fill uses BoundPlan in plan 19-16.

Purpose: D-01 as corrected by research F2: TradePlan has no symbol or side field, so the binding rests on the persisted Signal; symbol and direction are NOT added to plan_hash (D051 item c, one-way door already walked). Every mismatch is NO_TRADE with a reason code, before any adapter call.

Output: src/execution/binding.py, a public plan-hash wrapper in src/core/planner.py pinned by a golden test, and the binding test grid.
</objective>
