<objective>
Teach the reconcile tick about feed health and stream health (D-04, D-25, D058) and make it the real producer of two OPS-05 alerts: stale data and authentication failure.

Purpose: execution fails closed when the data it needs for risk or protection is stale; a dead user-data stream is acted on; an auth failure is visible to the operator.

The feed and stream sources are injected Protocols; Phase 24 binds them to the supervisor and the mark-price reader.

Executor rules: `codegraph explore "ReconcileLoop.tick StreamDivergenceKind StreamDivergence REASON_TASK_DIED StreamState classify ErrorFacts CallKind"` first; run only this plan's tests; stage explicit paths; commit trailer = the model that did the work.
</objective>
