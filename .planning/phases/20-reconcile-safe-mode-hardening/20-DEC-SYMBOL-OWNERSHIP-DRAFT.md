Proposed text:
1. Symbol ownership is checked at reconcile time only. Plan ingress is not changed: no plan is rejected at ingress because another plan is active on the same symbol.
2. More than one active plan for one symbol, or exposure on Binance whose owning plan cannot be determined, is the divergence `AMBIGUOUS_POSITION_OWNER`. It enters the global SAFE_MODE with the reason `SYMBOL_OWNERSHIP_CONFLICT`, which blocks new entries system-wide while monitoring, reconciliation, protective and reduce-only orders continue (D034). An active plan is a tracked plan whose protection state is not FLAT or ENTRY_ENDED_NO_FILL, or an untracked plan named by an unresolved (SENT or UNKNOWN) ENTRY intent.
3. Binance is authoritative for positions and orders. The local evidence of ownership is the durable execution intents plus the PROTECTION_TRANSITION journal replayed into the coordinator state (rebuilt per D-08). Local evidence is never sufficient alone to pick an owner.
4. Ambiguity, and unreadable or stale local data, fail closed: the tick makes no ownership decision on a failed read (SAFE_MODE `DB_FAILURE`), is skipped while the restart rebuild is not complete, and never picks one of two plans.
5. The conflict never moves a plan to CLOSE_REQUIRED by itself and never closes, cancels or guesses: the action is alert only (`ALERT_ONLY`), the alert event is `DIVERGENCE`.
6. The D077 protection rules (the stop deadline, the confirmed-then-lost rule, the close flow) apply to each plan independently, whether or not a conflict is open on its symbol.
7. This is a NEW policy. It is not part of D-13 and is not attributed to it.

- Do the local fields suffice? The 20-20 tests show that `plan_hash` and `symbol` of the tracked plans and of the unresolved ENTRY rows suffice to detect a second active plan on a symbol. The side and the intended quantity are not part of that check; they matter only for the exposure-based ambiguity (plan 20-04 fields `plan_hash`, `expected_side`, `max_abs_amount`), and that bound is not exact reconciliation (D-01a). Not shown, `[NOT_TESTED]`: a plan known only to the journal and not rebuilt into `protections()` with no unresolved ENTRY row; a plan known only by an unresolved stop or take-profit row; an unresolved row that wrongly leaves a tracked FLAT plan inactive. Their exposure still appears on Binance as `UNSYNCED_POSITION` or an unowned order, but the second-plan conflict is not detected from local data alone (`20-20-SUMMARY.md`, "Ownership source evaluation", gaps 1-6).

Reasoning: the per-symbol bound check of D-01a (the exchange position may not exceed the intended quantity of its plan, on the right side) is valid only while one plan owns a symbol; with two plans the same position cannot be attributed, and the lower-risk answer is to stop new entries and alert, not to guess. Failing closed costs at worst a missed trade. Detecting at reconcile time only keeps plan ingress unchanged, so no accepted plan is silently refused.
