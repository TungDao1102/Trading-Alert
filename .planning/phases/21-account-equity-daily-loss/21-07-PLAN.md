<objective>
The live equity source (criterion 1): the one object that turns the adapter's account reads into a fresh `AccountEquityReading`, keeps it only until the stream says the account changed, and resolves it to `AccountState` or NO_TRADE with the owner's age bound.

Purpose: D-01 says REST is the truth and ACCOUNT_UPDATE is the freshness path; `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` carries only wallet deltas and per-position unrealized PnL, so equity cannot be rebuilt from the stream (21-RESEARCH a): any account event or stream gap invalidates and a REST read is required. The source is a library; nothing constructs it in a running process until Phase 24.
