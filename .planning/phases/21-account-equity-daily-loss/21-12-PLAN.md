<objective>
Atomic risk reservation at approval (criterion 3, F-407, F-955, D-16, D-17): a durable `risk_reservations` table, a store with explicit lifecycle transitions, and a composed `SaveAtomic` that decides the approval and reserves the risk in one transaction under a single row lock.

Purpose: today the caps are checked on a caller-supplied snapshot with no lock and no reservation, so two concurrent approvals can both pass (F-407). The plan's risk is registered at issue time as a REQUESTED row (not counted) so the approval binds to the risk the operator saw; the approval flips it to RESERVED (counted) only if the shared gate passes inside the same transaction.
Design choices (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT): a REQUESTED row instead of a new approvals column, so the Approval entity, its request identity (D078) and the PgApprovalStore used by paper SHADOW stay unchanged; the lock is `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on today's `daily_risk_days` row (one operator, one process; A6); decide first, then reserve, so a lost race still surfaces as ApprovalAlreadyDecided and a refusal rolls the decision back.
Output: migration 0011, ORM row, store, composition, the optional hook in PgApprovalStore, PostgreSQL tests, pins and DATA_MODEL.

Reversibility: costly (D-16): touches the approval transaction and adds a persisted schema; the hook is optional and the downgrade drops the table. Not one-way, so no checkpoint.

Pins this plan updates: `tests/test_persistence_migration_pg.py` head pin to 0011_risk_reservations plus the 0011 import-guard, revises-0010, upgrade/downgrade and ORM-parity cases.
