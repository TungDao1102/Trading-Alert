<objective>
The stream path of the live daily-loss service (criterion 2, D-09): fills from the user-data stream feed the day loss between REST backfills, any ACCOUNT_UPDATE or stream gap makes the loss unknown until the next backfill, and the recorded Demo SL-triggered close is proven to reach the loss.

Purpose: the stream is the freshness path and REST the arbiter (D-01, D-09).
