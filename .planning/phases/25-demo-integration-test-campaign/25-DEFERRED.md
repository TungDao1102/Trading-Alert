# Phase 25 deferred register

Written by plan 25-17. One row per inherited item of the ROADMAP Phase 25 entry (section A), one row per
finding labelled F1.. in the plan SUMMARYs 25-01 to 25-16 (section B), and a few smaller open points those
summaries record (section C). `tests/test_phase25_gate.py` parses the ROADMAP entry and the SUMMARYs and
fails when an inherited id or a finding has no row here, when a status is not one of the four words below,
when a strict xfail twin is not named, or when a row claims a Demo or mainnet result without an evidence
path.

Status words:

- covered: built and tested by the named plan or scenario with fakes or the project's own stores. Covered
  never means observed on Demo: every Demo result stays NOT_OBSERVED until the owner runs the scenario
  (CONTEXT D-01) and plan 25-20 records it.
- owner-open: only the owner can decide or do it. This register records the question and the facts; it
  chooses nothing. No row here decides OWNER-1 to OWNER-4, R-21, R-22 or the -1003 margin key.
- deferred: a named later phase, plan or review, or kept on purpose (D-14 for R-23 and R-28).
- superseded: replaced by a later accepted decision.

Nothing in this file claims that any step ran on Demo or mainnet. The twins of plans 25-08 to 25-16 are
SYNTHETIC; the existing Demo evidence they build on is cited in `docs/operations/DEMO_VS_MAINNET.md`
(rows DVM-nn) and the Phase 24 smoke file. No requirement is marked complete here.
