<objective>
Close finding WR-01 of the Phase 19 review (`.planning/phases/19-execution-plan-binding-price-check/19-REVIEW.md`, section "WR-01"), with the owner's option (a): store the approved quantity on the Approval row (Alembic migration under `src/db/alembic`) and make `src/execution/binding.py::bind_plan` compare `trade_plan.quantity_after_rounding` against it, failing closed, so a placement quantity is never larger than the quantity recorded when the approval was issued. The plan-hash field set does NOT change (D051 (c)).

Today `bind_plan` copies the caller-supplied plan's `quantity_after_rounding` into `BoundPlan.approved_quantity`, and gate 5b of `execute_approved_plan` trusts it. That violates Phase 19 D-01 ("re-derived and compared, not trusted") and weakens D-02 (the placement quantity is at most the approved quantity).

Choices the owner delegated to the planner (recorded for the owner in REVIEW-FIX batch 4 and in D078):
1. **Plain Decimal, not a hash.** The bound is an order comparison (never more than approved), and a hash can only answer equality. NUMERIC(38,18), the project's money and quantity type.
2. **Nullable column; older rows refuse.** NULL means "no quantity recorded" (every row written before revision 0006). `bind_plan` refuses it as `APPROVED_QUANTITY_MISSING` before any adapter call. There is no backfill: copying from `trade_plans` would claim a recorded approval quantity that no record proves. This follows the convention of 0005: nullable only for "value never recorded", with a named CHECK.
3. **Equality at binding.** The plan's `quantity_after_rounding` must EQUAL the stored value (Decimal numeric equality, so a NUMERIC round trip still matches), else `PLAN_QUANTITY_MISMATCH`. The "at most" freedom stays where D-02 put it: gate 5b, the live-sized quantity against the stored approved quantity.
4. **Two distinct reason codes** (not a reuse of `QUANTITY_ABOVE_APPROVED`), so a refused older row cannot be mistaken for tampering.

Purpose: a TradePlan object with an inflated quantity can no longer pass every hash and approval check and place a larger order than the one recorded at approval time.
Output: commits `test(19): WR-01 ...`, `fix(19): WR-01 ...` (Task 1), `test(19)` / `fix(19)` (Task 2) and `docs(19): D078 ...` (Task 3). Nothing is pushed.
</objective>
